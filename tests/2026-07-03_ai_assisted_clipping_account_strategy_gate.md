# AI-Assisted Clipping Account Strategy Gate

Date: 2026-07-03
Zone: Incubator
Stage: Evidence Pass / Account Strategy
Status: Active planning note

## Purpose

Decide whether the clipping test should use existing aged social accounts or a separate legitimate clipping identity before linking accounts, joining campaigns, or posting clips.

This note is sanitized. It does not include exact handles, login credentials, private account details, payment details, private dashboards, source media, or sensitive metadata.

## User-Provided Context

- Existing accounts cover TikTok, Instagram, X, YouTube, Twitch, and less-relevant gaming platforms.
- The accounts use same or near-same naming across platforms.
- They were likely created around 2020.
- They have little or no following/view history.
- They are not believed to be publicly tied to the user's personal identity, though private account setup may use real identity information.
- User prefers not to use these accounts unless the improvement is meaningful.

## Key Platform Factors

- Content Rewards requires real social media accounts and prohibits fake identities, duplicate accounts, or burner accounts meant to cheat metrics or payouts.
- Content Rewards Trust Score can be helped by account longevity but can also be harmed by rejected submissions, suspicious bot scores, policy violations, clawbacks, and inactivity.
- Content Rewards may blacklist connected social accounts after serious violations.
- Montana gambling promotion/legal risk should be treated conservatively during the first test.
- FTC-style disclosure obligations are expected for paid or materially connected promotion.

## Recommendation

Do not use the user's existing aged same-name account set for the first campaign by default.

Use one of these safer strategies:

1. Preferred: create or select a separate legitimate clipping identity after the first campaign shortlist is chosen.
2. Acceptable: use one low-value social account first, not the full cross-platform identity, if a campaign requires a specific platform and the account risk is acceptable.
3. Avoid: connecting the user's reserved creator/streaming identity to a platform or campaign before campaign rules, risk category, and payout mechanics are clear.

## What "Separate Brand" Means

A separate brand means a legitimate content identity for clipping that is distinct from the user's future personal creator/streaming identity.

It does not mean a fake person, bot account, spam account, or disposable burner. It should be an authentic account identity with consistent naming, a simple bio, and honest disclosure behavior.

## What "Clean Campaign" Means

A clean first campaign is one with:

- Explicit source rights or approved assets.
- Clear campaign brief inside the platform.
- Clear payout rules and minimum thresholds.
- Clear rejection rules.
- Required disclosures that can be followed.
- No gambling, high-risk finance/trading, health-treatment claims, adult-ish content, or other category risks for the first test.
- No paid course requirement.
- No requirement to mislead, exaggerate, hide sponsorship, spam, or use fake engagement.

## Near-Term Decision

Before joining or linking accounts:

1. Audit campaign categories and requirements.
2. Exclude gambling and other legally/location-sensitive categories from the first campaign.
3. Pick 1-3 low-risk campaign candidates.
4. Decide whether a separate clipping identity can satisfy that campaign's account requirements.
5. Only then connect accounts or create clips.
